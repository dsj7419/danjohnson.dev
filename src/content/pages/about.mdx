## Background

I am a product engineer and systems architect focused on building complex, high-reliability software systems, AI-enabled products, and performance-critical applications.

I work with founders, designers, and small teams as a technical partner on serious builds — not a contractor who completes tasks, but an engineer who helps design, build, and deliver important software systems.

## What I Care About

Architecture-first thinking. Long-term maintainability. Security as a design principle, not a plugin. Performance as a feature, not an afterthought.

I believe the best software is built by people who understand both the product and the system — who can think about what a user needs and how the underlying architecture supports it reliably over time.

## How I Work

I work best on projects where engineering quality matters. Projects where the architecture needs to be right, where performance is critical, where AI integration needs to be thoughtful, or where a designer's vision needs a strong technical partner to bring it to life.

Every system I build follows the same principles: clear architecture, strict type safety, comprehensive testing, and honest documentation of decisions and tradeoffs.
