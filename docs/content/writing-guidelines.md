# Writing Guidelines

## Purpose

Writing on this platform is authority-building, not content marketing. Every article should demonstrate how Dan thinks about engineering, not just what tools he uses.

## Categories

| Category                 | Focus                                    |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- |
| `engineering-philosophy` | How to think about building systems      |
| `architecture`           | System design decisions and patterns     |
| `ai`                     | Real AI integration, not demos           |
| `lessons-learned`        | Honest retrospectives from real projects |
| `performance`            | Optimization strategy and measurement    |
| `security`               | Security as a design principle           |

## Required Frontmatter

```yaml
title: 'Article Title'
slug: 'article-slug'
description: 'One-sentence summary.'
date: 'YYYY-MM-DD'
tags: ['architecture', 'engineering']
category: 'engineering-philosophy'
draft: false
```

## Style

- Short paragraphs, clear headings
- Real examples and real tradeoffs
- Diagrams where they clarify
- No buzzwords, no trend-chasing, no "Top 10" lists
- Tone: senior engineer explaining to another smart person

## Frequency

Quality over quantity. 1 strong article per month or per major project. A single excellent article can generate opportunities for years.
